# Recreate QuotaDock

[中文](recreate-prompt.md) | **English**

Build a macOS quota overlay with Swift/AppKit. Place a 36×104 pt glass capsule above the ChatGPT/Codex avatar, with a 70 pt gap above the avatar and roughly 20 pt separation from the current native usage ring. Read local Codex JSONL quota receipts every 5 seconds while visible, otherwise every 30 seconds. Do not launch the CLI or call quota APIs. Select the latest codex observation by timestamp, preferring five-hour otherwise weekly limits and excluding Spark. Represent remaining quota with the liquid height, two surface waves, and rising bubbles. At the bottom, show only a whole number, with no percent sign or background plate. Use a white up-to-15 pt monospaced heavy font with a diffuse black shadow, shrinking long values to leave space at the sides. Use cyan for at least 50%, amber for 20% to below 50%, and red below 20%. Animate level changes over 0.6 seconds, pause when the target window is hidden, and keep the liquid still when Reduce Motion is enabled. Allow newer observations to increase or decrease. Persist the last receipt per log directory and restore it after restart. After 15 minutes, keep the number, show a small clock and pause bubbles. Show unknown only without a reading or after reset. Failed reads must not erase an in-period cache, and older observations must not replace newer ones. Support fractional timestamps and truncated tails without treating file modification time as observation time. Document that logs may contain multiple accounts. Include tests and repeatable build, signing, and packaging scripts.
